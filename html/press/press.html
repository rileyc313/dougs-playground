Description

Short: Doug's Playground is a cozy top-down fishing & exploration game. Wash up on an oddly shaped island, learn its waters, and get tangled up with the Muck... a race of mercury blobs who all share one body under whatever hat makes them somebody. One of them is named Doug, banished away nearly 650 years ago.

Long: The island sits atop an ancient kingdom, reachable only through a well inside it's castle. Its people, the Muck, are one being made of “powder of infinite proportion”... no lineage, no death, just a reconstruction of what once was. Doug “The Banished” is the only Muck who ever learned to feel joy, which made him a problem for a species that moves as one mind. King Roland banished him to Fishy Island, hoping the spirit of the mainland's villagers would spark a new flame of invigoration.

As the player, you strike a deal with Roland: offer items to the well! Saltwater fish, freshwater fish, wool, plates of pickled prawns, whatever the mainland will actually notice. And, in return, he sends back the pieces of Doug's playground, one at a time. The slide, the seesaw, the monkey bars. Raise it all back up, and maybe mend what got broken along the way.

Multiple save slots + autosave. All character and creature art is original, hand-drawn work.

Features

  • Fishing that fights backCast, wait for the bite, then reel against the fish's pull before the line snaps. 30+ species across freshwater and saltwater, each with its own weight, fight and rarity roll: Normal, Silver, Gold and Exotic.
  • The FindexEvery fish, tool and piece of junk you've ever pulled up, logged and searchable by habitat, with descriptions to boot.
  • Make somebody upCharacter creation with independently swappable bases, hair, eyes and every clothing layer, a favorite color the game remembers, and a companion who tags along for the journey.
  • A village that talks backBranching dialogue, quests and a reputation system. Some villagers won't tell you their real name until they trust you.
  • More than a fishing gameCooking, foraging, crab traps, pig and sheep pens, a coin economy and a general store stocked with questionable pickles.
  • Your own pocket dimensionA little home you decorate — chests, shelves and more.
  • A world with weather to itDay/night cycle, beach music that fades in when you hit the sand, and trees that go translucent as you walk behind them.
  • Quests“Doug's Dilemma,” “Onward, and Upward,” “Tailor Made,” “Stuck in a Pickle,” “I NEEEED IT!!” — with more on the way.
